## Goal
Replace the single 40×30 region with an OSRS-style seamless world: one continuous map split into chunks
that load and draw only around the player, with a few distinct areas (the starting village, a forest, a
lake, a second village, room for a mine). No loading screens; the minimap, pathfinding, saving and the camera
work across chunk borders; phones stay smooth.
